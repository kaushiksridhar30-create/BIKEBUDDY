Your bike should talk to you. So I built one that does. 🏍️

Meet **Bike Buddy**, a voice-first riding AI built on @Gnani.ai's Prisma (speech-to-text) and Timbre (text-to-speech) for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge.

🗣️ Ask in Hinglish, English, Hindi or Kannada: "petrol kitna hai?", "tyre pressure?", "service kab due hai?"
🔧 It checks your bike before you ride and speaks first when something's wrong.
🚨 Crash Guard: it senses an impact, asks "kya aap theek hain?", and if you say "madad" or stay silent, it alerts your contact and speaks to people nearby.
🧠 Your buddy even has a name you choose.

Built in under two weeks: Flask, three.js, a throttled Gnani API client, fuzzy word matching for mis-heard speech, and a responder dashboard with an impact graph.

Honest note: it's a prototype. Bike data and alerts are simulated, and the next step is plugging into real bike telemetry. Today it's an app. Tomorrow it could ship inside every bike.
